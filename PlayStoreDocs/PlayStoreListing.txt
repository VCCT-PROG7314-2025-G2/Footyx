# FootyX – Google Play Store Listing

## App Title
**FootyX – Live Football Stats**

---

## Short Description
Live scores, fixtures, stats & favourites for players, teams, and leagues.

---

## Full Description

**FootyX** is a modern football companion app built for fans who want fast, accurate, and personalized football information. Stay updated with real-time scores, fixtures, player stats, team performance, and league standings — all in one clean and intuitive app.

Whether you follow one team or multiple leagues, FootyX gives you the tools to track everything happening across the football world.

---

## Features

### **Live Football Data**
Get real-time match results, upcoming fixtures, detailed match events, standings, and season statistics from API-Football.

### **Powerful Search**
Search for:
- Players  
- Teams  
- Leagues  
- Matches  

Fast and accurate football data at your fingertips.

### **Player & Team Statistics**
Explore detailed stats including:
- Goals, assists, cards  
- Team rankings  
- Match performance  
- League data  
- Player career profiles  

### **Favourites**
Save:
- Favourite teams  
- Favourite players  

Quick access stored securely on your device.

### **Secure Firebase Authentication**
Register and log in using Google’s secure Firebase Authentication.  
FootyX never stores your password.

---

## Privacy & Security
FootyX uses Firebase Authentication and secure data handling practices.  
We do **not** share or sell personal information.
