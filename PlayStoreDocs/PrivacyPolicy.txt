# Privacy Policy for FootyX

FootyX ("the App") provides live football scores, fixtures, team information, player statistics, and personalized features such as favourites. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

By using FootyX, you agree to the terms described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Personal Information
We collect the following personal information for account creation and authentication:
- **Email address**
- **Password**  
  (Handled securely by Firebase Authentication.)

### 1.2 Usage Data
We collect limited non-personal information to support core app functionality:
- Favourite players and teams (stored **locally** on your device)
- Basic data stored in **Firebase** for authentication-related operations

### 1.3 Third-Party Football Data
FootyX retrieves football data (teams, leagues, players, matches) from **API-Football (API-Sports)**.

No personal user data is shared with API-Football.

---

## 2. How We Use Information

We use collected data to:
- Authenticate users securely using Firebase
- Provide access to app features
- Store favourites locally for personalization
- Display football data from API-Football
- Improve performance and user experience

---

## 3. Data Storage and Security

### 3.1 Firebase Storage
Your authentication data is stored securely using:
- Firebase Authentication
- Firebase Database

Firebase uses industry-standard encryption and secure infrastructure.

### 3.2 Local Storage
The following information is stored only on your device:
- Favourited teams  
- Favourited players  

This data never leaves your device.

---

## 4. Data Sharing

FootyX **does not sell, rent, or share** user data with third parties.  
The only external connection is to **API-Football**, used solely for retrieving football content.

No personal information is transmitted to API-Football.

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy

FootyX is **not intended for children under 13**.  
We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

---

## 6. Your Data Rights

You may:
- Request your account be deleted  
- Request deletion of your Firebase data  
- Update authentication details  

---

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy as necessary. Changes will appear on this page with an updated date.
